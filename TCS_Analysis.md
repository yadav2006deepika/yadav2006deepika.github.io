# Case Study: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
## Financial Forensics & Contract Analysis

### 1. Business Model & Economic Resilience
* **Core Revenue Driver**: Contract-driven model based on **Percentage Completion Ratio** for long-term IT projects.
* **Resilience Profile**: Moderate. Unlike FMCG, this model is tied to client IT budgets. During recessionary cycles, companies may postpone "discretionary" digital transformation projects, creating revenue risk compared to staple-goods models.

### 2. Structural Integrity & Accounting
* **Operational Lens**: Revenue recognition relies on estimates of project completion. 
* **Forensic Focus**: Wrong estimations in the "Percentage Completion" model can lead to significant revenue leakage or misleading profit reporting. Auditors prioritize this as a Key Audit Matter to ensure "True and Fair View."

### 3. Governance & Risk
* **Governance**: Robust internal controls are critical. Given the long-term nature of contracts, management’s ability to estimate costs accurately is the primary indicator of operational health.
