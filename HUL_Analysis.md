# Case Study: Hindustan Unilever Ltd. (HUL)
## Financial Forensics & Structural Analysis

### 1. Business Model & Economic Resilience
* **Core Revenue Driver**: Volume-driven FMCG business.
* **Resilience Profile**: High. The company’s portfolio (soaps, detergents, tea, coffee) is characterized by **inelastic demand**, making the business model highly resilient during economic downturns/recessions compared to discretionary-spend models.

### 2. Structural Integrity (The "Detective's Audit")
* **Observation**: Financial comparability between FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26 is impacted by the **demerger of the Ice-Cream business**.
* **Analytical Adjustment**: A standard year-over-year (YoY) comparison is misleading. To maintain "Apples-to-Apples" integrity, analysis must isolate "Continuing Operations" and account for the loss of synergy or changes in operational focus post-demerger.

### 3. Risk & Governance (ESG Integration)
* **Key Audit Matters (KAM)**: Auditors flagged "Revenue Recognition" and "Impairment of Assets" due to high management judgment requirements. 
* **ESG as Financial Leverage**: 
    * **Environmental**: Focus on renewable energy and waste reduction acts as a direct margin-optimization lever by lowering long-term energy and production costs.
    * **Social**: High gender diversity (44% managerial base) and CSR focus correlate with stronger brand trust, higher employee retention, and lower overall staffing costs.
    * **Governance**: Ethical controls act as a shield against regulatory litigation, providing long-term investor confidence.
