# Reliance Industries Limited (RIL): Comprehensive Financial Analysis (FY 2025-26)

## 1. Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) for the fiscal year 2025-26. By bridging the gap between macroeconomic narratives and audited financial performance, this analysis evaluates RIL's ability to navigate an evolving economic landscape, characterized by global volatility and domestic growth.

---

## 2. Key Financial Performance Metrics (Consolidated)
RIL maintained strong financial discipline and growth momentum throughout the fiscal year.

| Metric | Amount (₹ crore) | Growth (Y-o-Y) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Revenue** | 11,75,919 | 9.8% |
| **EBITDA** | 2,07,911 | 13.4% |
| **Cash Profit** | 1,71,258 | 16.6% |
| **Profit After Tax (PAT)** | 95,754 | 17.8% |

*Note: FY 2025-26 consolidated financials include ₹8,924 crore from the sale of listed investments.*

---

## 3. Capital Expenditure (CapEx)
*   **Total Capital Expenditure:** RIL's capital expenditure for FY 2025-26 reached **₹1,44,271 crore** (US$ 15.2 billion), compared to ₹1,31,107 crore in the previous financial year.
*   **Funding:** Capital requirements were primarily funded through the company's internal cash profits, maintaining a strong balance sheet.

---

## 4. Segmental EBITDA Performance
Consumer-facing businesses now contribute over 55% of RIL's consolidated EBITDA, highlighting a strategic shift toward digital and service-based revenue.

| Segment | EBITDA (₹ crore) | EBITDA Growth | Key Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Digital Services** | 76,560 | +17.8% | 5G adoption, subscriber growth, 241 exabytes data usage. |
| **Oil to Chemicals (O2C)** | 60,546 | +10.1% | Strong transportation fuel cracks and operational scale. |
| **Retail** | 27,034 | +7.7% | Strategic investment phase in hyperlocal commerce. |
| **Oil & Gas** | 19,050 | -10.1% | Expected natural production decline in KG D6 block. |
| **Media & Entertainment** | 5,842 | +218.7% | High-engagement content (Cricket/TV) & ad-tech monetization. |

---

## 5. Key Financial Ratios
The company’s focus on operational efficiency and debt management is reflected in its strong performance ratios.

*   **Debt Service Coverage Ratio:** Improved to 4.03 (up from 2.06 in FY 2024-25).
*   **Interest Coverage Ratio:** Rose to 8.83 (up from 5.59 in FY 2024-25).
*   **Net Profit Margin:** Improved to 8.1% (up from 7.6% in FY 2024-25).
*   **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** Increased to 20.7% (up from 14.6% in FY 2024-25).
*   **Net Debt to EBITDA:** Remained significantly below 1x, supported by superior earnings quality and balance sheet flexibility.

---

## 6. Analyst Verdict
Reliance Industries has demonstrated resilience as a **diversified growth engine**. The company's ability to maintain a consolidated EBITDA growth of 13.4% confirms that internal strategic execution—specifically in its consumer-facing digital and media segments—remains the primary driver of shareholder value.

> **Analyst Note:** RIL successfully navigated a "stampede" of high commodity prices and currency depreciation by leveraging its internal ecosystem, proving that its competitive moat is built on technological scale and aggressive, long-term capital allocation.
